Getting started

Cloud Songs is a free web music player. You can search for songs, browse editorial playlists by language, play full tracks, and keep a recently-played history. It runs entirely in your browser on both phones and laptops — there's nothing to install.

Yes. Searching and streaming are completely free. You only need a free account so your profile and sign-in work across devices.

No. Cloud Songs works in any modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). If you'd like an app-like shortcut, open the site and use your browser's Add to Home Screen (mobile) or Install option (desktop).

Any up-to-date browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. The layout adapts automatically — phones get a bottom tab bar (Home, Playlists, Recently played), while laptops show the same controls in a top bar and the bottom player.

Account and sign-in

Open Log in, switch to the Sign Up tab, then enter your name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters. We'll email you a 6-digit code — enter it to verify your email, then set up your profile and you're in.

Verification confirms the address is really yours and keeps your account secure. After signing up you'll see a code-entry screen; the code we email is valid for 10 minutes.

  • Check your Spam or Promotions folder.
  • Give it up to a minute to arrive.
  • Make sure the email address was typed correctly.
  • Use the Resend code button (available again every 30 seconds).

Codes expire after 10 minutes and allow a few tries before they're locked. Just tap Resend code to get a fresh one and enter the newest code.

Self-service password reset isn't available yet. In the meantime, please reach out through the Community/contact link below and we'll help you regain access.

If an account was created but never verified, logging in sends a fresh code and takes you to the verify screen to finish. Sessions also expire after about 30 days, after which you'll sign in again.

Yes. Accounts are kept on our server, so you can sign in on your phone, tablet, and computer with the same email and password.

On the marketing pages, open the profile menu in the top-right and choose Log out. Inside the player, open the account chip in the top-right and select Log out.

These are set on the profile screen right after your first verification. You can revisit it any time you're signed in to update your display name, avatar colour, and short bio.

Playing music

Search for a song in the top search box, or open a language and tap any track in the list. It starts playing in the bottom mini-player. Tap the mini-player to open the full-screen now-playing view.

Type a song or artist name in the search box at the top. You can also paste a JioSaavn song or playlist link and Cloud Songs will resolve it for you.

  • Play / Pause and Next / Previous to move through the queue.
  • Shuffle plays the list in a random order.
  • Repeat cycles through off → repeat all → repeat one.
  • Volume and the seek bar to jump within a track.

Tap the mini-player at the bottom to open the full-screen view with large artwork and controls. It only closes when you press the × (or use Back), so it won't disappear from a stray tap while music is playing.

Song lists load a few tracks at first. Show More reveals additional tracks, and once the current list runs out it automatically pulls in more songs from related playlists so you can keep listening.

Playlists and languages

Open Playlists (the bottom tab on phones, or the nav on laptops) and pick a language to see its latest chart and editorial playlists. Tap any playlist to open its songs.

The catalogue includes Hindi, English, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Kannada, and Malayalam. Choose a language from the home screen to load its playlists.

Open the Recently played tab. It lists the songs you've played on this device, most recent first.

Back steps through the app one layer at a time — from the now-playing view to the playlists, to a language's songs, and back to the language list — before it leaves the page. The logo/Home returns you to the language picker without stopping playback.

Privacy and security

Your account details (name, email, and a securely hashed password), your profile (display name, avatar colour, bio), and your recently-played list, which is kept in your own browser.

Passwords are hashed on the server and never stored in plain text. Your browser only holds a secure, signed session cookie — not your password.

Yes — the only email we send is your 6-digit verification code. We will never ask for your password by email. If a message asks for your password or payment details, it isn't from us.

Troubleshooting

Streaming links can expire; when that happens the player automatically fetches a fresh one and retries. If a track still won't play, try another song or refresh the page.

Your browser may be showing a cached copy. Do a hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac). On mobile, pull down to refresh or clear the site data.

Cloud Songs needs an internet connection to fetch the catalogue. Check your connection and try again. If search is empty, try a different spelling or a shorter query.

Reset your browser zoom to 100% with Ctrl+0 (Windows) or Cmd+0 (Mac). The layout is designed to fit the screen at normal zoom.

Long titles wrap onto multiple lines so the full name is visible. If it still looks off, a hard refresh will load the latest layout.
